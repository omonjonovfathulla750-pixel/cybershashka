INTERNATIONAL 10×10 RULES

TACTICAL SPECIFICATION & COMBAT LOGIC

1. THE 10×10 MATRIX & PIECES

The game is played on a 10×10 board using the 50 dark squares. Each commander starts with 20 pieces (Player 1 Cyan on bottom, Player 2 Magenta on top).

2. REGULAR MEN MOVEMENT & JUMPS

Regular pieces step forward diagonally by 1 square. Crucially in International Draughts, regular men can capture diagonally both FORWARD and BACKWARD by jumping over an enemy piece into an empty square.

3. MANDATORY CAPTURES & MAJORITY RULE

Capturing is strictly mandatory. If one or more jumps are possible, the player cannot make a simple move. If multiple jump chains exist, the move capturing the maximum number of enemy pieces must be executed.

4. CHAIN CAPTURES & REMOVAL

Multi-jumps must be completed in continuous succession within the same turn. Captured pieces are dissolved after the entire chain sequence concludes.

5. FLYING KINGS & OVERDRIVE PROMOTION

When a piece finishes its turn on the opposing baseline, it triggers an Overdrive Promotion into a Flying King. Kings can travel any distance diagonally and capture enemy pieces across long diagonals.

6. TURN TIMER & MILTILLASH

Each turn features a 30-second countdown. At 5 seconds remaining, Miltillash Emergency Warning activates with intense visual pulsing and audio alerts. Reaching 0 auto-passes the turn.
